about local transcription

trove transcribes audio and video using whisper.cpp, running entirely on your machine. your media never leaves your computer.

the first time you transcribe, trove downloads a small AI model (~140 MB by default) from huggingface. this happens once — after that, transcription works without any internet connection.

you'll be able to pick the model size, see your machine's capability, and review what's downloaded before it starts.
