how to use

▸ Editing

Click anywhere in the document to place your cursor. Type to fix a transcript. Edits autosave.

⌘Z / ⌘⇧Z
undo / redo
Enter
split paragraph at cursor
Backspace
(at start) merge with previous paragraph
Esc
close menus / clear selection

▸ Playback

Space
play / pause
J / L
seek −5s / +5s
, / .
speed −0.25× / +0.25×
Click a word
place text cursor (no seek)
Double-click a word
seek to that word
Alt + click
seek without entering text-edit
Click [00:14]
seek to segment start

▸ Find & replace

⌘F
search transcript
⌘⇧F
find & replace (bulk)
Enter / ⇧Enter
next / previous match

▸ Speakers

Speakers are detected automatically (best-effort) and labeled Speaker 1, Speaker 2, …. Click any label to rename — the new name propagates to every occurrence of that speaker.

▸ Selection actions

Select any text to reveal a small toolbar: copy · highlight · bookmark · add note. Right-click a selection to also see export selection and revert paragraph.

▸ Bookmarks & notes

⌘B
bookmark current playback time

Notes anchor to a word; click the marker to edit or delete.

▸ Reviewed

Tick the checkbox in the gutter to mark a paragraph reviewed. Reviewed paragraphs get a faint green left edge.

▸ Video controls

Floating window: drag to reposition; ✕ to hide; ⛶ to expand to a fixed left rail (the document reflows beside it). Show video: use the ▸ show video button in the toolbar when hidden.

▸ Export

Use the ⬇ export menu for .txt, .srt, or .vtt. Includes any edits.

▸ Tips

  • Toggle follow to auto-scroll with playback.
  • The little ↓ jump to current button appears when you scroll away from the active paragraph.
  • Press ? any time to reopen this panel.
